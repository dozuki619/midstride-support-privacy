### Common questions

**Is my data backed up anywhere?**
No - Midstride stores everything only on your device, with no account or cloud sync. If you're switching phones, use your device's own backup/transfer tools (e.g. iCloud device backup or Quick Start) before setting up the new one, since we have no copy of your data to restore from. Midstride also includes its own local backup and restore option in Settings, which lets you export your data to a file you control (Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, etc.) and restore from it later.

**Does Midstride require an internet connection?**
No, the app works fully offline.

**I purchased Midstride but it's showing as locked on a new device — what do I do?**
Use the Restore Purchases option (available in Settings, and on the unlock screen) to re-apply your purchase. Since Midstride has no account system, purchases are tied to your Apple ID rather than a login, so Restore Purchases is how they carry over to a new device or reinstall.

**How do I request a feature or report a bug?**
Email us at the address above with as much detail as you can — what happened, what you expected, and your iOS/device version if it's a bug.
